# Image asset prompts

Mode: built-in image generation. No CLI/API fallback used.

All seven illustrations are saved in `public/images/`. The hero is landscape; individual sweets are square. No real shop stock is implied.

## Hero

Use case: ads-marketing. An exquisite photorealistic 3D-rendered luxury Indian mithai food campaign image, landscape. An antique brushed brass shallow thali filled with orange motichoor ladoo with pistachio, diamond kaju katli with silver leaf, delicate saffron peda, amber jalebi coils and dark glossy gulab jamun. Slightly elevated three-quarter view, dramatic but soft studio daylight, ivory warm seamless backdrop, hints of pink rose petals and pistachios, elegant high-end food styling. All sweets realistically textured and appetizing, no text, no logos, no hands, no packaging. Platter occupies center with breathing room.

## Product prompt format

Use case: product-mockup. Square premium food catalog image of [subject], on small ivory ceramic plate against uniform warm beige background. Closeup three-quarter angle, luxury photorealistic 3D food render, soft studio light, authentic appetizing texture. No text, logos or extra foods.

Subjects:

- Ladoo: 4 orange Indian motichoor ladoo, visibly delicate tiny boondi pearls, pistachio slivers.
- Katli: 5 diamond-shaped Indian kaju katli stacked elegantly, pale creamy cashew fudge with delicate edible silver leaf.
- Mysore pak: 4 golden rectangular pieces Indian ghee Mysore pak showing crumbly porous layers, neatly stacked.
- Jamun: 4 deep golden-brown Indian gulab jamun in glistening amber syrup in a shallow bowl with pistachio garnish.
- Jalebi: a small stack of orange Indian jalebi spirals, thin intertwined coils with glossy syrup.
- Peda: 4 Indian kesar peda, creamy pale yellow round flattened milk sweets with indented centers and saffron threads and pistachio garnish.
